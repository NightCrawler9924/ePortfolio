01Analog signal path

Amplifier Linearization with Learned Predistortion

UBC undergraduate research · Sept 2026 to present · in progress, no measured results yet

Stage under testLTC6078 op amp, 10 V/V, diode clipped at ±1 V
Dominant distortioncubic (third harmonic)
Correctionpolynomial predistorter, coefficients fit in Python from DC sweeps
AdaptationSDR captures output, re-fits inverse model as temperature and load drift
Design targetthird-harmonic reduction of 10 dB
ToolsLTspice, Python, software-defined radio

A diode-clipped op amp stage distorts once it passes its compression knee, and the dominant term is cubic. I am designing a polynomial predistortion stage that applies the inverse of that curve ahead of the amplifier so the chain stays linear further into the knee. The model lives in LTspice; the coefficients are fit in Python from DC sweep data rather than derived by hand.

Fixed coefficients drift with temperature and load, so the second half is an SDR feedback loop that captures the amplifier's actual output and re-fits the inverse with a learned predistorter. This entry gets numbers when the loop closes.

02Register transfer level

FaultLatchedPWM

Solo · 2026 · Verilog HDL on Tang Nano 20K FPGA

Functiondeterministic PWM motor controller with latched fault handling
State machineINIT, IDLE, RUN, FAULT, SAFE
Fault responseoutput held low, latched until operator reset
Config guardseparate module rejects out-of-range parameters at load
Flow completedsimulation, synthesis, place-and-route, physical board

When a fault asserts, the output drops to a safe state and stays there until an operator resets it, so a transient glitch cannot quietly put the drive back in motion. A separate configuration-validation module rejects out-of-range parameters at load time rather than letting bad settings reach the driver stage. Carried through the complete flow, from simulation to a physical board.

The Hailo compiler refused the model. I traced it to an incompatible automatic postprocess-extraction step and rebuilt the export path with explicit end-node parsing and a custom NMS configuration rather than working around it. The camera calibration replaced a placeholder focal length that every prior distance measurement had silently depended on.

Bugs fixed on the real unit: a SIGTERM handler bypassed by a vendored library, a non-daemon thread that hung shutdown, a race between the alert thread and hardware cleanup (confirmed with a 20-trial stress test, fixed with a lock and idempotent teardown), a flaky I2C connector, and an apparent startup hang that was stdout block-buffering.
