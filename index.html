Amplifier Linearization with Learned Predistortion

UBC undergraduate research · Sept 2026 to present · LTspice, Python, software-defined radio

StageLTC6078 op amp
Gain10 V/V
Clip limit±1 V
H3 target−10 dB
StatusIn progress

A diode-clipped op amp stage distorts once it passes its compression knee, and the dominant term is cubic. I am designing a polynomial predistortion stage that applies the inverse of that curve ahead of the amplifier so the chain stays linear further into the knee. The model lives in LTspice; the coefficients are fit in Python from DC sweep data rather than derived by hand.

Fixed coefficients drift with temperature and load, so the second half is an SDR feedback loop that captures the amplifier's actual output and re-fits the inverse with a learned predistorter. The target is a 10 dB drop in third-harmonic distortion. There are no measured numbers yet. This card gets them when the loop closes.

Wildlife-Vehicle Collision Early Warning System

UBC undergraduate research · May 2026 to present · Python, YOLOv8, Raspberry Pi 5, Hailo-8L NPU

mAP500.823
mAP50-950.638
Calibration1.34 px reproj.
Cold boot to detect~45 s
Unit tests47, hardware-free
Dataset7 cls, 2,860 img

A real-time system that detects animals near roadways and warns drivers, running entirely on embedded hardware. I built the 7-class dataset from scratch (3,015 images sourced, filtered to 2,860, split on a fixed seed), fine-tuned YOLOv8n, then quantized to INT8 for the Hailo-8L. The compiler refused the model; I traced it to an incompatible automatic postprocess-extraction step and rebuilt the export path with explicit end-node parsing and a custom NMS configuration rather than working around it.

Distance estimation uses thin-lens geometry fused conservatively with a time-to-contact optical-expansion estimate, calibrated against the physically mounted camera with OpenCV to 1.34 px reprojection error, replacing a placeholder focal length every prior measurement had silently depended on. The pipeline ships as a self-recovering systemd service verified by cold boot, not a warm restart. Bugs fixed on the real unit: a SIGTERM handler bypassed by a vendored library, a thread teardown race caught with a 20-trial stress test, a flaky I2C connector. Its 47 unit tests run without any hardware attached through a simulation backend.
